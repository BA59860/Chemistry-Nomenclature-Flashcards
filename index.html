Formula formatting
Choose a style, then type or tap numbers and signs. Select existing digits to reformat them. Use subscripts for atom counts (H₂O), superscripts for charges (SO₄²⁻), and Normal for hydrate coefficients (·5H₂O). Letters and parentheses stay normal.
Typing and scoring tips

Names ignore capitalization and extra spaces, including spaces around Roman numerals. Bicarbonate and hydrogen carbonate are both accepted.

The formatting buttons change typed numbers and signs, or reformat a selection. Normal returns to ordinary typing. You can also tap the on-screen number and symbol keys. Each new card starts in Normal.

Formulas accept ordinary digits or subscripts. Element capitalization, subscripts, parentheses, and ion charges must be correct. For example: Ca3(PO4)2 or Ca₃(PO₄)₂. For ions, use SO4^2- or SO₄²⁻, and NH4+ or NH₄⁺. Include (aq) for acids. For hydrates, use a middle dot or a period: CuSO4·5H2O or CuSO4.5H2O.

Correct counts first-try answers. Missed counts a card when you first get it wrong; retries do not add misses. A correct retry helps you learn but does not change that card’s score. Streak counts consecutive first-try answers and resets on a miss. Each card and direction is scored once per session. Skips are unscored.

Scores stay with you when you change categories, directions, or modes. New session clears scores and restarts the deck and timer. Scores stay only in this open page.
